# The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time

The Switch 2 base dump reaches its title screen on the Mac. VideoToolbox decodes the 1920×1080 AV1 track. The movie library runs its NDVE compute shaders to produce the background behind the logo and menu. Gameplay validation is open.

## Profile

SDK 22.2.8, NVN API 231.40, with Nintendo's sead, agl and gsys. The three loaded modules are `main`, `subsdk0` (NintendoSDK_movie), and `sdk`. The NPDM declares 48 MiB of system resources. Startup requests a 6.20 GiB heap, which fits in the profile's 12 GiB reservation with the stack and alias regions. The registered build is the supplied base dump (`v0`). `vk_prewarm.bin` is the Android host's prewarm seed (dm/games/README.md).

## Mac dry run

Use the rewritten `patched_v0` and the original extracted `base/romfs`. Build the host with `make -C dm/host GAME=0400486024DEC000` and use the [Mac shader build](../../../docs/development.md#build-by-hand-mac). From the repository, set `DUMP` to the folder containing `base` and `WORK` to the build and run workspace beside it.

```sh
mkdir -p "$WORK/run/save" "$WORK/run/temp" "$WORK/font"
python3 dm/tools/make_ext_font.py "$WORK/font/nintendo_ext.ttf"
DM_ROOT="$WORK" DM_ROMFS="$DUMP/base/romfs" DM_SAVE="$WORK/run/save" \
  DM_TEMP="$WORK/run/temp" DM_MSL_DIR="$WORK/shader/cache" DM_FONT_DIR="$WORK/font" \
  DM_NVN_SHMAP="$PWD/dm/games/0400486024DEC000/shader_map.tsv" \
  DM_WINDOW=1 DM_METAL_HUD=1 DM_AUDIO=1 \
  dm/host/dmhost "$WORK/patched_v0"
```

The profile prewarms the 11 NDVE compute pipelines used by the title. On the Apple M5 that took 17.7 s on a cold run, with a progress screen, and 7 ms with the archive warm. Compiling the same pipelines during the first submission had blocked the first movie frame for tens of seconds. The larger scene shader set also triggers a separate graphics-pipeline prebuild. On the first run with that set, the startup screen took 86.9 s to hand out all pairs, and the remaining workers finished after 110.6 s total.

Start a new game to reproduce the opening scene transition. With the test save and title flow, `DM_KEYS=f2600:A,f2950:A` advances the menu automatically. `DM_NVN_SHDUMP` saves control sections and code for missing keys to the directory it names.

For a run past the opening, copy the committed save folder to the new run's `DM_SAVE` and load slot 00 from the menu. The checkpoint contains both `slot_00/progress.sav` and `slot_00/caption.sav`. The load screen identifies it as **Kokiri Forest Autosave**. A windowed reload reached the playable scene with the HUD and controls, so rendering can be tested without replaying the movie.

## Found on this game

- Its modules are [ZBIC-compressed](../../../docs/reference/packing.md#zbic-compressed-nso-segments): Prepare on 0.2.1 stopped at the rewrite, on the phone and in a fresh PC or Mac environment, because neither had a ZBIC decoder (resolved in 0.2.2: the APK carries libzbicdec, PC and Mac environments the zbic package).
- [Zero-sharing capped at 32 MiB leaves out the large targets it was made for](../../../docs/reference/memory.md#zero-sharing-capped-at-32-mib-leaves-out-the-large-targets-it-was-made-for-android): its giant depth duplication led to the 32 MiB cap (a319e773); which images, open.
- The online library's [eventfd for its poller](../../../docs/reference/services.md#eventfds-served-offline) and the [virtual audio input list](../../../docs/reference/audio.md#no-virtual-audio-input-devices-sdk-22) blocked startup.
- The SDK's [NDVE pack](../../../docs/reference/shaders.md#the-movie-librarys-ndve-shader-pack) contains 22 compute programs. The title route uses 11.
- The [raw write log and pool lookup](../../../docs/reference/frame-rate.md#a-full-frame-shader-overflows-the-raw-write-log) took the windowed title movie from a 47.6 ms median to 16.67 ms on an Apple M5, at 1920×1080 decode. A steady 300-frame window averaged 60.0 fps and its GPU median was 15.94 ms. A simultaneous ten-worker shader build caused additional stalls. The same run returned to 60.0 fps when the build paused.
- A [play report send](../../../docs/reference/services.md#an-asynchronous-play-report-opens-prepou) aborted at the first gameplay transition after a failed `prepo:u` connection. A Mac dry run with the offline completion finished the 9,254-picture opening movie and continued submitting the live scene. The title-only shader set left the scene black.
- The white 3D scene was agl's [conditional depth clears](../../../docs/reference/rendering.md#conditional-rendering-evaluated-at-replay) run unconditionally (resolved; the profile sets `render_cond on`). The main opaque pass tests `EQUAL` against a copy of the prepass depth that agl clears under a condition right after copying, a clear the console skips. Without the key the copy was 1.0, no lit geometry passed, and the full-screen sky (`943d8c0ece545cc8`) showed through. With the key the saved Kokiri Forest scene renders: Link, the ground, the props, lighting and the HUD.
- Mesh-shaded models were missing, among them the door of Link's house: the split read six stage slots of each BNSH program, and NVN2 keeps [task and mesh shaders in slots 7 and 8](../../../docs/reference/shaders.md#task-and-mesh-shaders-in-nvn2-bnsh-program-slots-7-and-8) (resolved). The new split adds 491 mesh and 17 task shaders to the map, 53 of the 54 keys the Kokiri Forest scene had asked for. 511 of their 520 build; the door renders.
- Kokiri Forest ran at about 20 fps with the GPU two-thirds idle: the [bindless pool table was rebuilt for every registration](../../../docs/reference/frame-rate.md#a-bindless-pool-table-rebuilt-for-every-registration), and about 12,000 descriptors of finalized texture objects churned the ghost ring (resolved). The frame is now 16–21 ms at the median on an Apple M5, submit 5–8 ms, and the GPU (14–21 ms) is the limit.
- The first 3D scene exhausted Metal memory when [bindless pool walks churned finalized textures](../../../docs/reference/nvn-objects.md#a-bindless-pool-churns-finalized-textures-within-a-submission). On the Apple M5, the failing run reached 18,930 MiB of residency. With submission-local backing, residency was 7,946 MiB about ten seconds after the movie and 7,871 MiB at 35 seconds. The opening 3D scene and its subtitles presented, and the test host exited normally.
- On Android (Snapdragon 8 Elite / Adreno 840, Turnip v32), the AV1 title movie compute shader `d15370a382630d4e` triggered Android LMK kills (7.5 GiB RSS) at frame 79 due to Turnip inlining 768 `GlobalToStorage` helper calls; [resolved by branchless single-block SPIR-V helpers](../../../docs/reference/shaders.md#global-memory-helper-explosion-under-turnip-inlining) in `vk_android_spv.py`.
- The fog's light classifier `9e6b00b050e30801` read 0 through every pointer loaded from memory past the pool table's 64th entry: a merge had brought back the [pool table search cut to 6 steps](../../../docs/reference/shaders.md#the-pool-table-search-cut-back-to-6-steps-by-a-merge) (cut removed, Vulkan chain `6d96e42cd517`). With the whole search unrolled, mesh shaders `73dc0b90c4082e43` and `eed1add7c89f5d34` (637 helper calls) aborted Qualcomm's compiler (`std::bad_alloc`, frame about 1,800); the search is now a loop the driver does not unroll (Vulkan chain `72c5210a46e5`, library `v-72c5210a46e5`): they compile in 7.0 and 7.5 s, and the white veil is gone (resolved 2026-11-08).
- After the restamped library `v-0035e16073bf` replaced `v-72c5210a46e5` (no shader changed), the 2.9 MB pipeline cache was thrown away and the first start rebuilt every pipeline on a black loading screen for minutes: [a shader library update that threw the pipeline cache away](../../../docs/reference/memory.md#a-shader-library-update-that-threw-the-pipeline-cache-away-android) (fixed, not checked on the phone yet).
- A cold first 3D frame (one submission of about a minute of pipeline creation) was never saved in the pipeline cache when the run stopped during it, so every 110 s run started cold again: [a pipeline cache that never saved a cold first 3D frame](../../../docs/reference/memory.md#a-pipeline-cache-that-never-saved-a-cold-first-3d-frame-android) (resolved).
- The long waits after the intro and main menu (one 3D frame of 50-112 s, then frames of 2-17 s) were the scene's pipelines compiled one after the other on the replay thread, mesh pipelines (3-17 s each on Qualcomm's driver) never made ahead: [a first start compiled a whole scene's pipelines in one frame](../../../docs/reference/rendering.md#a-first-start-compiled-a-whole-scenes-pipelines-in-one-frame-on-the-vulkan-replay). - Storage image views for sRGB textures failed with binding shape mismatches; [resolved by allowing UNORM storage views on sRGB-compatible textures](../../../docs/reference/rendering.md#storage-image-views-for-textures-with-srgb-format-lists-on-the-vulkan-replay) in `vka_tex.c`.
- Integer texture class reinterpretation (resolved in `vka_tex.c`): allowed format list learning (`vl_learn`, `vl_bytes`) and `id_view` class twin conversion (`class_twin`) across integer textures (`kind <= 2`), allowing `NVN_FORMAT_R16G16_UINT` (VkFormat 81) textures to be bound as float shader storage images (`VK_FORMAT_R16G16_SFLOAT`, VkFormat 83) without reading as unbound.
- Multi-serial image eviction use-after-free (resolved in `vka_tex.c`): added `map_del_im` to invalidate all secondary `g_map` texture cache entries when a shared `vka_img` is pruned or evicted, preventing dangling pointer dereferences.
- Indirect mesh draw commands (resolved in `hle_nvn.c`, `vka.h`, `vka_enc.c`, `vka_draw.c`): implemented `nvnCommandBufferDrawMeshTasksIndirect` routing through command buffer recording (`p_draw.ind`) and executed via `vkCmdDrawMeshTasksIndirectEXT`.
- Texture cache eviction thrashing (resolved in `vka_tex.c`): raised default `DM_VK_IMG_LIMIT_MB` from 512 to 1536 MiB; at 512 MiB with a ~1,000 MiB scene working set, `vka_tex_prune_locked` forced `min_age = 1`, evicting ~250 MiB of active textures per frame and dropping the frame rate to 1.0–1.5 FPS under continuous re-uploads in `0x105d Optimize`.
- NVN2 variable-rate shading (`BindShadingRateImage`, `ShadingRateImagePalette`, `SetShadingRatePaletteMode`) and workload priority calls registered as no-ops in `hle_nvn.c` (`g_nops`).
- Kokiri Forest 3D gameplay renders at 59.4 fps (16.95 ms frame time), submitting 2,643 draws per frame in 4.96 ms GPU time with 3,003 MiB placed memory on Mac. On Snapdragon 8 Elite / Adreno 840 (Turnip v32, Android 17 / SDK 37), the frame loop runs steadily past frame 2000+ with audio running smoothly, but visual rendering presents black/empty because Turnip v32 does not support `VK_EXT_mesh_shader`, skipping NVN2 mesh draws.

## Open

- Resolved (2026-11-07): the Kokiri Forest false colours; see Found on this game (R32_UINT shader-image views without storage use). Its investigation: view reinterpretation and float images bound to integer textures were ruled out; the NDSR network sampled serial 107213 (RGB9E5) that still held R11G11B10 bits, because a335's `r32ui` writes were dropped.
- No HUD on the phone in the run with library `v-39c415842121`: no hearts, minimap or text 4 and 18 s after frame 4,500, and the HUD pair mesh `147550d4e76629d5` with pixel `442da04b508b2667` was never asked for. With the older library the hearts and minimap drew, and that pair failed pipeline creation with -13 (the pixel module lacked `PerPrimitiveEXT`; a freshly built pixel module with any mesh shader compiles in the probe). Whether the HUD was simply not on screen yet is open: one run only.
- GPU time on the phone (Qualcomm's driver) after the upscaler fix (Found): 18.8-20.4 ms a frame at 50-51 fps. The upscaler takes 6.5-7.7 ms, the render passes 8.7-9.3, and steps with nothing recorded (barriers, uploads, writebacks) 10-11. The scene stayed at 854×480 for the 20 s run because the frame is still slower than 16.7 ms. Open: what fills those 10 ms of unrecorded steps.
- Open (2026-11-08): the over-bright picture on the phone (dark shadows, sunlit ground near white, a white veil; the title's forest scene shows it from frame 1,446). agl's scene analysis is not the cause as far as it runs in the title: with `DM_VK_SB_PEEK` (Qualcomm's driver, frames 1,446-1,495 and 2,200-2,244) the compute shaders `77c4bfc0d562c310`, `278f48371bcf0d58`, `424b9852065ec581`, `1bba09ec4bc8ff67`, `21d9f83e6027c565` and `6e28359aa3558f61` leave non-zero sums in their storage buffers once their submissions complete, the next frame's dispatch finds the previous sums still there (nothing clears them before the CPU could read them), and the values change smoothly from frame to frame (`77c4`'s word 5 settles at about -3.83, which `6e28`'s output repeats in its word 7). Every 5 to 6 frames at first (2,200, 2,205, 2,211) all of them hold their start values for one frame (256 and 1.0, 0, 48 of 48): not explained. Not checked: the analysis in gameplay (Kokiri Forest), where the probe and shadow analyses also run, and what the lighting reads of these results (missing ambient or probe lighting is the remaining lead). The analysis's float atomics are translated: every RED.ADD.F32 of the 34 shaders that have one gets the compare-and-swap loop. Checked since (2026-11-08, Qualcomm's driver, `DM_VK_TEX_PEEK` / `DM_VK_SB_WATCH`): every texture slot of the 142 logged scene shaders gets a texture; the cube probes are drawn face by face into their layers and levels and sampled from the same image (serials 4136, 4153, 4312, 4313, all non-dark down to 1x1); the 192-byte buffer `1bba09ec4bc8ff67` and `21d9f83e6027c565` add into (the probe and sky luminance, words 32-35 about 0.09, 0.06, 0.02, 0.066 in the cutscene) is read in place by every lit material shader, and nothing on the GPU reads `6e28359aa3558f61`'s or `77c4bfc0d562c310`'s outputs (the CPU's). `77c4`'s word 5 is a sample's view depth clamped at -10, not an exposure. The translator's float min, max and clamp are NaN-defined (`vk_android_spv.py` makes every `FMin` / `FMax` / `FClamp` `NMin` / `NMax` / `NClamp`), and OoT has no float atomic min or max. Still open: where the white veil and the burnt sunlit ground come from (the froxel fog `5b2bb0924ecc403b` adds the veil).
- Resolved as no fault (2026-11-08, `DM_VK_RANGE_WATCH` and `DM_PROBE_WATCH`, Qualcomm's driver): words 4-11 of the 192-byte lighting buffer the lit shaders read (2^-10 in gameplay, about 0.003 at moments of the title) are written by the game's CPU, not a shader or a readback: main+0x337608 (in the build of `game.tsv`) stores `colour × scale` twice and clears words 12-35 (the sums `1bba09ec4bc8ff67` and `21d9f83e6027c565` add into) once a frame. The scale is `2^-EV` (main+0x3ae6b8, `exp2f`) of the environment's exposure parameter (main+0x3ba5b0 blends two parameter sets), EV 10 in the Deku Tree cutscene and Kokiri Forest: an authored value, not a clamp of a measured one, so the exposure is not what makes the picture too bright.
